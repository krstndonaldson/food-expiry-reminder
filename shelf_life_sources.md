# Shelf life sources

The `Shelf life` sheet in `food_tracker_template.xlsx` uses the numbers below. Each one has a source so you can check it yourself.

**These are general guidelines, not food-safety advice. The package label always wins.**

## How the numbers were chosen

- Where a source gives a range, I used the **low end**, so the reminder comes early rather than late.
- "Days once opened" counts from the day the item is opened. For produce, eggs and pantry items I count from the day it was bought.
- Health Canada covers dairy, eggs, hot dogs and leftovers. The produce and onion numbers come from Love Food Hate Waste Canada, which is **not** a government source.

## Where sources disagree

**Opened milk.** Health Canada says 3 days. Some other charts, including a US university one, list 7 days. I used Health Canada's number because it is stricter and it is the Canadian authority.

## Not covered

I did not find a clear Health Canada number for cut fruit and vegetables, so they are left out rather than guessed. If you add your own items, write where the number came from in the Source column.

*Sources checked October 2026. Guidance can change, so check the links if you are relying on a number.*
